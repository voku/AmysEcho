## Agent Task Brief
Goal: a caregiver opens the caregiver area by a deliberate press-and-hold with a visible countdown (no password), sees the stored profiles (and a calm note when entries are damaged), creates a profile with a name, makes one active, and chooses and orders catalogue concepts for it; closing returns to the child's board, which shows the change without a reload. German text. Uses the exported app controller (store(), refreshBoard()), writes boards only through setBoardConcepts with the profile's full conceptIds, and offers 'Tafel neu laden' as the retry after a refresh time-out. Not in this slice: rewording, custom concepts, export/import, delete, repair of damaged entries, profile switching protection beyond the hold gesture. Scope: src/caregiver.ts, src/main.ts, public/, test/caregiver.test.mjs, test/bootstrap.test.mjs, docs/caregiver.md, README.md. Depends on: NG-28.
