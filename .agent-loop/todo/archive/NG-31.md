## Agent Task Brief
Goal: a caregiver sets up and maintains a child's board quickly and without surprises. 1. A header that stays at the top with the title, which board the child will see after closing, and a 'Fertig' button; Escape closes too. 2. Profiles as cards with clear actions: use as active board, edit board, rename, delete — delete asks again and says what is lost and that there is no backup yet. 3. The board editor is two lists: 'Auf der Tafel' in board order with move up, move down and remove, and 'Weitere Begriffe' with add; nothing moves except the row that was acted on; the last removal can be undone. Locked rows keep their place. 4. The create form shows one short line; the full notice about what is stored is folded away but one tap from the field. 5. A warning when a board has more tiles than fit comfortably on a phone; a hint where a German voice is installed when the device has none. 6. 'Tafel neu laden' and other rarely needed things sit in a help section. 7. The area's no-board line no longer asks for a page reload. Verified by the implementer in real Chromium at 360x640, 390x844 and 820x1180. Scope: src/caregiver.ts, public/, test/, docs/caregiver.md, docs/board.md, docs/storage.md, README.md. Depends on: NG-29, NG-30.
