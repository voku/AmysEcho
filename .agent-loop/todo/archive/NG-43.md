## Agent Task Brief
Goal: the caregiver can choose the front or the back camera in the teach view, the camera test and the child's recognise view (or a device setting used by all three). The choice is remembered on the device. The preview is mirrored for the front camera only (a photo-app view for the back camera). The tracker keeps getting the camera's own frames; left must keep meaning the child's own left hand with either camera — verify how getUserMedia delivers front and back frames (neither is mirrored by the browser) and that handedness labels stay correct; examples taught with one camera must match attempts with the other. A device without a back camera, or a refused switch, gives a German message and falls back.
