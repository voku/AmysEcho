## Agent Task Brief
Goal: a written decision with numbers: tracker (MediaPipe Hand Landmarker is the candidate), model and wasm asset sizes, licence, worker support, frames per second and time to first result under CPU throttling in headless Chromium, and whether pose landmarks (shoulders) are affordable or the hands-only anchor is used. Also decide two input rules the matcher needs: left/right convention with front-camera mirroring, and conversion of normalised coordinates to equal scale on both axes. Probe code stays in build/evidence/NG-16/probes/ and is not committed. Scope: docs/decisions/hand-tracking.md, docs/recognition.md. Depends on: nothing. Phone numbers stay marked unverified until NG-6 style device evidence exists.
