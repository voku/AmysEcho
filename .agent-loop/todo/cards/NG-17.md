## Agent Task Brief
Goal: a developer or carer can record tracker output (landmark frames, never video) on their own device into an ignored local directory and replay it through features, segmentation and matcher with one command, so capture changes are checked against real jitter, dropouts and handedness flips. Nothing recorded is committed: AGENTS.md and issue 1176 require public fixtures to be synthetic, and landmark data of a real person is personal data. CI therefore stays on synthetic fixtures; the synthetic generator is extended with the fault patterns seen in real replays (documented as observations, without data). Committing real landmark recordings, even of a consenting adult, would need an explicit owner policy change first and is out of scope. Scope: scripts/, src/recognition/synthetic.ts, test/, .gitignore, docs/recognition.md. Depends on: NG-3.
