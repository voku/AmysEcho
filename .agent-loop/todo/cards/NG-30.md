## Agent Task Brief
Goal: the child's screen is a board, not a page. 1. A sentence bar at the top shows the last sentence in large type and repeats the speech when tapped; it replaces the retry button. 2. The board fills the rest of the viewport without scrolling for the usual number of tiles; columns follow the number of tiles and the orientation; symbols and labels scale. 3. A tapped tile shows it at once (pressed state) and stays marked as the selected one. 4. Speech status is shown only when there is a problem, in one quiet line. 5. The app name and explanatory text take no space from the board; the examples frame and badge stay but cost no column on a 360 px screen. 6. Touch behaviour: no text selection or callout on tiles, no double-tap zoom, repeated taps on the same tile within a short time do not restart the speech. 7. scripts/offline-smoke.mjs serves every shipped module and asset. Verified by the implementer in real Chromium at 360x640, 390x844, 820x1180 and 844x390. Scope: src/board.ts, src/startup.ts, public/, scripts/offline-smoke.mjs, test/, docs/board.md, README.md.
