## Agent Task Brief
Goal: a caregiver creates a concept of their own (label and sentence), puts it on the board, moves and removes it, and deletes it for good after a confirmation. Uses putCustomConcept, removeCustomConcept and listCustomConcepts of src/board-data.ts. Scope: src/caregiver.ts, public/styles.css, public/sw.js, test/caregiver.test.mjs, docs/caregiver.md. Rules: generate concept ids, never derive them from the label; putCustomConcept followed by setBoardConcepts is two writes, so a failure in between must leave a state the area shows and can finish; never offer deletion of an entry a newer app wrote (unsupported-version), and listCustomConcepts().unreadableIds carries no damage kind, so add it before offering deletion of an unreadable one; ghost ids count toward the 500-id cap; the deletion follows the delete rules of docs/caregiver.md (takeover, hold, release). Non-goals: pictures for own concepts (NG-27), import of concept lists.
